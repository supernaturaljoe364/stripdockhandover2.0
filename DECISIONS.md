# Decisions

Choices made while building the Round 3 software, with reasons. The safety rules in the master prompt section 4 are not changed by anything here.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **Strip = eSHa AQUA QUICK TEST (7-in-1).** Nitrate chart blocks 0 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 mg/L. | Read from the bottle photos (`data/chart/`). The chart has a 500 block as well as the expected 0–250. |
| D-02 | **Units: mg/L NO₃⁻ (nitrate ion).** No conversion (`to_no3_factor: 1.0`). | Bottle label says "NO3 mg/L". Operator confirmed on 2026-11-19. NO₃-N conversion (×4.427) is implemented and tested in case another strip is used. |
| D-03 | **Read time 60 s.** | Bottle text "... 60 Sek.", confirmed by the operator. |
| D-04 | **Pad order:** nitrate = pad 1 at the tip (far from the handle), nitrite = pad 2, then GH×3, KH, pH, Cl₂ nearest the handle. | Read from the strip photo and the bottle's strip drawing, confirmed by the operator. In practice the regions are drawn by hand in calibration. |
| D-05 | **lighting_mode = phone_torch.** The torch is switched on by `lock_camera.py` and stays on all session. It is never flashed per shot (`flashmode=off`). | Operator's choice. Constant light keeps exposure stable. Glare from the on-axis torch is handled by the glare check (D-13). |
| D-06 | **Read endpoint `/shot.jpg`.** | `tools/phone_check.py` (2026-11-19, 10 frames each): `/shot.jpg` 1920×1080, latency 0.08 s mean / 0.12 s max, central-patch ΔE00 0.19 mean. `/photo.jpg` 1920×1080, 0.82 s mean / 1.57 s max, ΔE00 0.69. `/photoaf.jpg` is never used, because autofocus would undo the focus lock. Source: `results/phone_check.json`. Re-check once docked. |
| D-07 | **Read frames at T+0, 1.5, 3, 4.5, 6 s (T = 60 s)**, not T±3 s. | Decision rule 1 says "never earlier" than the read time. A 6 s window starting at T keeps the drift check ("still developing") without reading before 60 s. Configurable: `read_offsets_s`. |
| D-08 | **Camera lock without lock switches.** This phone's IP Webcam has no `exposure_lock` or `whitebalance_lock` (both return 404). **Exposure:** `manual_sensor=on` with fixed `exposure_ns`, `iso` and `frame_duration`, chosen by bisection so the brightest channel of the white patch sits at about 215/255. **White balance:** the fixed preset that makes the white patch most neutral (`daylight`). **Focus:** manual (`focusmode=off` + `focus_distance`) when the sweep reaches at least 0.8 × autofocus sharpness; otherwise **single-shot autofocus** (`focusmode=auto` + one `/focus` trigger at lock time, never at read time). On the dock, manual focus is clamped by IP Webcam at 10 diopters (10 cm) and is blurry (sharpness 1.4–2.2 against 12.8–35 with autofocus), so `single_af` is in use. The lens was measured to stay put after one trigger (sharpness 33.2 → 32.7 over 20 s). Because the lens position is not reported, `--check` also compares the live sharpness of the patches with the value at lock time (fail below 50%). | A fixed manual exposure is a stronger lock than AE-lock. Only settings the phone reports are touched. Frozen 2026-11-19 21:23: exposure 4.548 ms, ISO 25, daylight WB, single AF, torch on; white-patch stability ΔE00 0.18 mean, 0.22 max over 10 frames (`results/camera_lock_check.json`). |
| D-09 | **Frozen camera state** = `data/calib/camera_settings.json`: all reported settings that affect colour or geometry, plus `lighting_mode`. `--check` compares the live `/status.json` with it (numeric values within 2%). Zoom, orientation, mirror and colour effect are recorded and checked, never changed. | Training, testing and the app's START all require this check to pass. |
| D-10 | **ROI re-location:** template-match the white/grey/black patch row (phone or dock movement), and the strip anchor if one is drawn (strip movement), searching ±30 px. Fail if the shift is over 15 px or the match score is under 0.6, with the reason "strip not seated". Sample each region shrunk 20% from every edge. | Searching wider than the tolerance means a big move is measured and reported rather than silently capped. |
| D-11 | **Strip-present check** compares the pad region with an **empty-tray frame** taken during calibration: ΔE00 ≥ 6 **or** mean grey-level difference ≥ 12. Calibration refuses to save without the empty-tray frame. | The dock tray is black foam, so a white strip is easy to detect. Thresholds are refined from dry runs. |
| D-12 | **Colour correction:** linearise sRGB, apply a per-channel gain so white → 0.85, then, if stable (white > grey > black in every channel, span ≥ 0.05, fit residual ≤ 0.03), a per-channel affine fit through black, grey and white to their own neutral levels. Otherwise white gain only (the method used is logged per frame). Then CIELAB D65. Grey-patch chroma after correction > 8 → quality fail "reference patches inconsistent". | Transparent and needs no knowledge of the patches' true reflectance. Our model is trained on our own captures, so consistency matters more than absolute colorimetry. |
| D-13 | **Glare** = pad pixels with low chroma ((max−min)/max ≤ 0.12) that are brighter than min(250, max(240, white-patch level + 10)). Fail if > 2% of the pad. | Found in testing: a 0 mg/L pad is near-white, and an absolute "≥ 240" rule flagged it as glare (34% of pixels on a synthetic white pad). Specular glare is brighter than the matte white reference, so the level is relative to it. |
| D-14 | **Light leak** uses the raw (uncorrected) black-patch L*, monitored about once a second through the whole countdown plus the 5 read frames: fail if any frame is above `black_L_max` or any frame-to-frame jump exceeds `black_jump_max`. | The correction would hide a leak, so the raw value is used. Monitoring the whole countdown catches a drawer opened at any time. |
| D-15 | **Stale frame:** identical JPEG bytes on two consecutive grabs, or an HTTP latency above 2 s → quality fail. | IP Webcam can keep serving the last frame if the camera stalls. A live sensor never produces byte-identical JPEGs. |
| D-16 | **Model:** an ordered polyline through the per-standard mean Lab points (monotonic in concentration by construction). Projection uses the Euclidean distance in Lab onto each segment, the nearest segment is chosen by ΔE00, and concentration is interpolated in log(c + 1 mg/L). The reference method is nearest-centroid by ΔE00, log-interpolated with an adjacent runner-up. | Simple and transparent for about 21 strips, as the prompt asks. `+1` makes the 0 mg/L standard usable on a log scale. |
| D-17 | **Unknown rejection:** UNKNOWN if the distance to the model exceeds max(2.0, p99(leave-one-strip-out distances) × 1.5) ΔE00. | Leave-one-out distances are more honest than in-sample residuals. The 2.0 floor stops a tiny training spread from rejecting every new strip. |
| D-18 | **Borderline band:** HOLD when the estimate is ≥ exp(log(25 + 1) − 2 × RMS(LOSO log error)) − 1, the RMS taken over standards within 0.4×–2.5× of 25 mg/L (that is, 10, 25, 35, 45 and 55). | RMS includes systematic bias (strips reading low), not only spread. If the band reaches 10 mg/L, train.py warns that false holds are expected. It is not capped, because the band is a safety margin. |
| D-19 | **Primary method selection** by LOSO on training data only, lexicographically: fewest missed (true ≥ 25 read PASS), then fewest false holds + UNKNOWNs, then lowest mean \|log error\|. LOSO thresholds come from the full training set (slightly optimistic; this is stated in the model card). | Safety first, then usefulness, then accuracy. |
| D-20 | **Freezing:** the frozen model stores the ROI hash, camera-settings hash, quality thresholds and read time. Test, degraded and demo sessions refuse to open, and START is blocked, if any of them differ now. | Thresholds cannot be tuned after the model is frozen without it showing. |
| D-21 | **Models used per mode:** test, degraded and demo use only the frozen model. Train and dry use the frozen model if there is one, else the latest trained model, else none (with no model the read is UNKNOWN "no calibration model"). | Scored and filmed reads always use the hash-checked frozen model. |
| D-22 | **Training labels:** the concentration typed in the app (train mode) is cross-checked against `train_labels.csv` when that file has the sample. A mismatch makes training refuse. Reads that failed the quality gate are excluded and listed in the model card. | Catches typing errors. |
| D-23 | **Dry-run blanks:** dry-session reads noted "distilled" are only used as 0 mg/L training data with `train.py --include-dry-blanks`, and only after the operator confirms. | As the prompt requires. |
| D-24 | **Evaluation scores the verdict that was actually sent to the panel at read time.** A recomputation from the stored features with the frozen model is reported only as a consistency check. Sessions must be closed (read-only + SHA-256 manifest) and intact. | No re-scoring with different thresholds. |
| D-25 | **Baseline TDS rule for the "missed nitrate" comparison:** RO+UV only when TDS ≥ 300 ppm (Round 2 TDS-only logic, per the prompt's "meter TDS < 300"). Computed from the TDS column of the answer key. | Makes the "20/20 missed" baseline a measured number rather than a claim. |
| D-26 | **Timing:** "dip to verdict" is measured from the START press, which the operator makes immediately after dipping, blotting and sliding the tray in, up to the verdict. The dip-to-START gap is not measured by the app; the results file says so. | The read timer must start at START (rule 1). |
| D-27 | **Service panel** in the UI can send HOLD, UNKNOWN or STATUS by hand, never PASS. | Clearing a hold must need a real passing read plus the physical button. |
| D-28 | **Python 3.14.3** (the installed version, ≥ 3.11), own CIEDE2000 implementation (tested against Sharma et al. 2005 pairs), no scikit-image or scipy. | Fewer dependencies. Clopper–Pearson intervals are computed by bisection on the exact binomial CDF. |
| D-29 | **The HOLD LED is amber** in the sketch's wiring comment (the prompt says red). The UI shows HOLD in red. | No firmware change; noted only. |
| D-30 | **Known limitation: dry strip.** A dry, undipped nitrate pad is white and may read as 0 mg/L → PASS. Whether the pipeline can tell dry from wet will be measured in HIL step 6. Until then: **the operator must dip the strip; a dry strip can read as 0.** | Prompt Phase 10, step 6. |
| D-31 | **Reference patches as built (2026-11-19):** white = torn plain white paper (L\* 77.8 raw), "grey" = a marker-filled square on that paper (L\* 40 raw, chroma 3), black = marker scribble on paper (L\* 27 raw). All are glued to the tray floor below the strip. The regions avoid the patch edges and the glossy streak on the black patch. | They are neutral and ordered white > grey > black, so the 3-point correction is stable (residual ≤ 0.03). Weaknesses: marker ink has a slight sheen under the on-axis torch (black reads L\* 27, not ~10), and the "grey" is darker than an ideal L\*≈50 grey. **Before training starts** they may be swapped for a printed 50% grey and matte black card, followed by a recalibration. Never swap them after training starts. |
| D-32 | **Strip position:** horizontal across the top half of the frame, tip (nitrate pad) at the left, against the end-stop. Anchor region = the strip tip only, excluding the nitrate pad, whose colour changes when wet. | Template matching needs a part that does not change colour. |
| D-33 | **Panel link watchdog:** 3 PINGs in a row without a reply → close and reopen the port. Reopening resets the board, which reports its saved EEPROM state, so a hold is never lost. The reader thread logs and survives malformed lines. | Seen once on 2026-11-19: in one app start the board answered STATUS but not the PINGs that followed. It could not be reproduced in two later starts. The watchdog makes this case recover by itself instead of showing "not linked" forever. |
| D-34 | **Strip supply:** the scored tests (20 + 20 + 30 + 10), about 30 degraded reads and about 21 training strips need ~130 reads; one bottle holds 50 strips. Use the same lot (7833) for training and testing, or record `strip_lot` per read. | A change of lot is a known source of colour shift. |
| D-35 | **One retry per frame.** A frame request that fails or takes longer than `max_frame_age_s` (2 s) is retried once (`frame_retries: 1`). The read fails the phone check only if the retry also fails. Retries are counted in `meta.json` (`metrics.n_retries`). | On 2026-11-20 the Wi-Fi link dropped 10–25% of requests. A single blip should not waste a strip, but a phone that stays unreachable must still give UNKNOWN (unit tests cover both). |
| D-36 | **Phone link over USB:** `adb forward tcp:8080 tcp:8080`, with the phone at `http://127.0.0.1:8080` (`phone_ip: 127.0.0.1`, `adb_path` in config). The app re-creates the forward at startup and after a cable replug. | Wi-Fi measured 2026-11-20: 8 of 30 frames failed, including a ~30 s outage. Over USB: 30 of 30 frames in 0.06–0.11 s, and the cable also charges the phone (the torch is always on). The phone is a Samsung Galaxy S24 (SM-S921E). This replaces "Wi-Fi" in the prompt's table; the HTTP interface is unchanged. |
| D-37 | **Colour correction fixed to `white_gain`** (`correction_mode`, frozen into the model and checked in test mode). | After the layout change the marker "black" (raw L\* 41–44, shiny) and "grey" (L\* 49–55) were almost equal in the red channel (116 vs 120). The 3-point fit could flip between methods from frame to frame, which would add noise. The white-patch gain is applied the same way on every frame. Grey and black are still used by the checks. |
| D-38 | **Strip budget about 50; data collection reduced to one strip per level** (the team's decision, 2026-11-20). | The original plan needed about 130 strips. With one strip per level there is no estimate of strip-to-strip spread, so the rejection threshold and borderline band fall back to their floors. More strips per level are needed before the claims can be tested. `evaluate.py` judges targets as proportions and flags reduced sample sizes. |
| D-39 | **Strip-placement tolerance: proposed, not yet implemented.** Keep the patch-row tolerance at ±15 px (phone or dock movement), but allow a hand-placed strip to sit up to ±40 px from calibration when the tip anchor matches with score ≥ 0.75, and widen the search window to ±60 px. | 15 px is about 0.6 mm at this scale, tighter than a strip can be placed by hand without a physical end-stop. On 2026-11-20 strips landed 10–42 px from calibration, and the tray itself varied up to 30 px. A physical L-shaped stop for the strip and a hard stop for the tray are the better fix, and the team should build both. |
| D-40 | **Single-container ascending series** for 2026-11-20 (see `data/labels/collection_log_20261120.md`). Labels use the volume-corrected actual concentrations (9.84, 25.08, 35.07, 44.84, 55.06, 70.09, 100.11 mg/L). | The team had one container. Reading in ascending order ties time to concentration; this is stated as a limitation. |
| D-41 | **Strip plan for 22–24 Nov (28 strips, pre-registered in `data/labels/collection_plan_20261122.csv` before any read):** dry 3 (D1, W1, T1; E1–E3 empty), training 14 (0/10/25/35/45/55/100 mg/L × 2, randomised order seed 20261122, each level in its own cup), blind 9 (5 spiked tap 60–100 mg/L, 4 compliant 0–10 mg/L, coded by the chemistry person), degraded 15 reads reusing strips, demo 2. Replaces D-38's one strip per level and RUNBOOK §5's three per level. | The team counted 28 strips. The handover rule cuts unseen standards first, then the conservative pair; training never below 2 per level. Consequence: **no blind block-accuracy or conservative-zone test**; those rows are reported as not tested (LOSO on training data only). Agreed by the team in chat 2026-11-22. |
| D-42 | **Auto START** (`tools/auto_read.py`): the read is started by software the moment the reference patches and the strip tip are both found and still for 1 s, after the tray was seen without a strip. Empty-tray reads start immediately (`--now`). The quality gate is unchanged; a strip outside ±15 px still starts and is judged "not seated". | The operators only handle strips; waiting for a chat reply before START would add an unknown delay between dip and read. Auto START fires 1–2 s after the tray closes, which is what "press START at once" means in D-26. If the strip tip is more than 30 px off (outside the search window) it does not fire, and the read is started by hand. |
| D-43 | **Seated tolerance 25 px, search 50 px (was 15 / 30), and frozen into the model** (`roi_locate` in the model; test mode refuses if `roi:` in config.yaml changes; `tests/test_frozen_match.py`). Supersedes the D-39 proposal. | Team request 2026-11-22. Measured first: with the tray stop the tray returned within 0–2 px in 4 of 5 pushes (one at 14–17 px), but hand-placed strips landed 19, 39 and 81 px from the calibration because the team aligns the strip to pencil marks drawn after that calibration. The ROI was then recalibrated to the pencil-mark position (ROI `a4857fd2db689a0d`). Regions are re-located by template matching before sampling, so a 25 px (≈1 mm) shift still samples the pad; the torch light field is uniform at that scale. The 50 px window means larger shifts are measured, not clipped (D1 at −50 px was clipped at −30). Before this change the ROI tolerance was not part of the frozen model; now it is. |
| D-44 | **D-39 implemented (supersedes D-43's numbers):** patch row (tray) tolerance 30 px; strip tip may sit up to **40 px** off when its match score is ≥ 0.75 (≤ 30 px with the normal 0.6); search window 60 px. `app/roi.py` `locate(strip_tol, strip_min_score)` + `locate_cfg`; all callers use config. Frozen into the model with the other `roi:` settings. Tests: `test_strip_tolerance_wider_with_strong_match`; `test_phone_moved` now moves beyond the configured tolerance. 93 tests pass. | Measured after the team's tray stop and pencil marks (2026-11-22): tray within about ±20 px, strip within about ±20 px of the marks; W1 landed at tray (7, −20), strip (8, −39), tip match 0.95, and failed the 25 px rule although its pad region sat on the pad. The pad region follows the tip match, so the larger tolerance keeps the measurement on the pad; the stricter score guards against a false tip match. Set before any training read. |
| D-45 | **strip_min_dE lowered 7.0 → 3.0** (2026-11-22 14:33, during training, before any model or test). | The tray floor is pink, and the nitrate pad turns pinker with concentration: pad-vs-empty-tray ΔE00 fell 12.8 (0) → 12.1 (10) → 10.5 (25) → 8.6 (35 mg/L), so 45–100 mg/L strips would soon fail "strip present" and be excluded. Empty-tray reads never exceeded ΔE00 1.1, so 3.0 keeps a ≥ 2.7× margin for detecting a missing strip. Training data only; the session was closed and a new one opened so each session has one threshold set. |
| D-46 | **Training stopped after series A + S0-B (team decision 2026-11-22 ~14:52); model frozen 15:01** (`models/frozen/model_20261122_150113.json`, sha256 29933a5c…, primary curve, reject ΔE00 2.73, borderline HOLD from 4.5 mg/L, 8 reads). Series B (S10-B…S100-B) not collected; its 6 strips go to the blind test. | The team chose to skip the replicate series after being told the consequence (one strip per level except 0, so the LOSO spread is an estimate from single strips). Consequence, stated in the model card and to be reported: the borderline band reaches 4.5 mg/L, so compliant water at ~5–10 mg/L is expected to be HELD (false holds); LOSO: 0 missed, 0 false holds, 0 UNKNOWN, 4/8 within one block. Colour in 25–55 mg/L is flat and non-monotonic (a* 2.0 / 6.0 / 4.6 / 3.4). |
| D-47 | **Protocol change, version 2 (team decision 2026-11-23 ~15:10): black_L_max 27.5 → 40.0, white_L_max 83.2 → 90.0; model re-frozen as `models/frozen/model_20261123_151156.json`** (sha256 a2d5fe7e…). Same training reads, identical curve/thresholds/band (verified field by field); only `quality_thresholds` differ. All other limits unchanged, including the light-leak **jump** check (2.0 L* frame-to-frame). | After the reboot and a lens wipe on 23 Nov the dock read raw black L* ≈ 39.4 and white ≈ 83.5 with good sharpness (≈ 120), so every read would be refused. The team instructed these limits, stating the judges accepted the setup. **Version 1 (model_20261122_150113, limits 27.5/83.2) remains the model of record for the blind test of 22 Nov; those results are not re-scored.** Reads from 23 Nov on carry the v2 model hash and are reported separately (`evaluate.py --tag v2`). Consequence: the absolute light-leak level check is weaker; a leak that raises black slowly without a frame-to-frame jump > 2.0 would pass. |
