Region calibration

Do this only when the tray, phone or lid has moved. After training starts, never (a new ROI invalidates the model). Back to the main screen
  1. Pull the tray out, leave it EMPTY, slide it in, click Capture EMPTY tray.
  2. Put a strip (dry is fine) in the tray against the end-stop, slide it in, click Grab frame with strip.
  3. Pick a region button, then drag a rectangle on the picture. Repeat for every region. Draw inside the pad / patch, not over its edges.
  4. Click Save.
